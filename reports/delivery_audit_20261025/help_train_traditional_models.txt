usage: python.exe -m src.models.train_traditional_models [-h] [--quick]
                                                         [--out OUT]
                                                         [--max-train MAX_TRAIN]
                                                         [--max-test MAX_TEST]

Train and evaluate traditional ML models for four 5G KPI forecasting tasks.

options:
  -h, --help            show this help message and exit
  --quick               Run a reduced experiment for debugging. Do not use
                        quick-mode results in the final report.
  --out OUT             Optional isolated experiment directory; default keeps
                        legacy outputs.
  --max-train MAX_TRAIN
                        Maximum training rows in --quick mode only.
  --max-test MAX_TEST   Maximum test rows in --quick mode (0 = all).
